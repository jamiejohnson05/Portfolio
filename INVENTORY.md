## What the artwork actually shows

Rendered 45 of the `.ai` files to look at them rather than guess from filenames.

**Medal Designs 2023 is three things, not one.** Actual medals (Cerus Arena,
Division Winner, CerusBolt, Champion 1st/2nd/3rd, Fitness Festival, Trilogy),
race bibs (CerusArena, CerusBolt, Fitness Festival 5K — the "100 / $5 OFF MERCH
/ 100 BAG CLAIM" tear-off design), and one embroidered patch. The bibs are
`promotional`, not `medal-design`.

**The Trilogy medal is the standout object.** A three-piece medal — Gym Series,
Race Series, CerusBrix — that assembles into one hexagonal piece. `FINAL
Trilogy Medal 2023.ai` is the finished art. `Official Trilogy Split Medal
2023.ai` renders as a plain black hexagon, so it's a cut or template file, not
artwork.

**The two Untitled files are identified.** `Untitled-2.ai` is two Trilogy medal
variants stacked on one artboard. `Untitled-6.ai` is CerusBolt 2023 in a blue
Colorado-flag colorway. Neither is a separate project.

**NYX is a real identity, not a sketch.** An owl with a spread wingspan, worked
as mark alone and as a lockup with the wordmark, across black / cyan / dark /
light. Clean geometry, confident silhouette. `NYX White Logo` renders blank
here because it's white-on-transparent — it needs a dark ground, which this
site has.

**Merch 2026 is the strongest set.** The ombré smiley-kettlebell (checkerboard,
lightning bolts, peach-to-teal gradient) is the most current and most
distinctive thing in the folder. Mountain Athlete, Ransom Letters, and the
retro Cerus Fitness wordmark are all finished.

**The 2025 champion tee is a system.** Full tee layout with the athlete's name
and number set into it — `JOHNSON` / `04` — plus "EARN YOUR SPOT. PROVE IT ON
THE FLOOR." Twenty-two athletes, same frame. That's the piece that shows
systems thinking rather than decoration.

**Stackable Boxes are event floor graphics.** Wraps for plyo boxes, Cerus and
DEKA, each carrying a zone number and its movement — "30 RAM ALT REVERSE
LUNGES." The DEKA ones mean official race-format work. `banners` fits better
than `promotional`.

**CerusArena "Sections" are station signage** — "SECTION 5: Sled Push (200LB) /
Sled Pull (200LB) / 200' Male / 150' Female." Narrow horizontal strips, not
large-format repeater banners. Worth knowing before they go under `banners`.
